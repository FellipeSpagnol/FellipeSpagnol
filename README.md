## 👋 About Me

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at the Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES), researching robotics, dynamic control, and real-time distributed systems.

- 🔭 **Current Research:** Developing a **Digital Twin Framework in ROS 2** for heterogeneous multi-robot systems, focusing on continuous co-simulation (Gazebo) and closed-loop control integration.

- 🧠 **Research Interests:** Robotics, Industrial Automation, Control Systems, Machine Learning, and Reinforcement Learning.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.S. in Electrical Engineering – Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES).
